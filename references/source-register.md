## Research questions for the next review

- What is the latest status of the federal study, recommended plan, authorization, funding, and any subsequent design or construction?
- Which alternatives and engineering appendices specifically address reef placement, breakwater modification, wave transmission, circulation, sediment transport, and shoreline impacts?
- What are the breakwater's current as-built geometry, armor details, foundation conditions, inspection findings, and maintenance requirements?
- What baseline ecological surveys and sediment characterization are available for candidate segments?
- What current approvals and coordination are required from the structure owner, City, USACE, port/navigation stakeholders, and environmental agencies?

## Evidence handling

For each source, record title, issuing organization, publication date, URL, retrieval date, relevant pages/sections, and the claim it supports. Prefer primary agency reports and peer-reviewed research over summaries. Clearly distinguish source findings from project hypotheses and assumptions.

## Okinawa precedent

Research the specific Okinawa habitat-enhancing breakwater design being used as the precedent. Verify the study name, design details, measured outcomes, monitoring period, and transferability limitations from the primary publication before citing numerical ecological benefits. Okinawan coral-focused outcomes must not be assumed to transfer directly to Southern California species or conditions.
